How well it works

The models were fine-tuned on SIPaKMeD and then tested on Herlev, a dataset they never saw. Accuracy stays high on the training dataset and drops on a new one, and the drop is mostly dataset shift.

What to keep in mind

  • The models were trained on one dataset of single-cell crops (SIPaKMeD) with two labels, normal and abnormal. Metaplastic cells count as normal.
  • On a different dataset (Herlev) accuracy fell to 51% to 79% and calibration error grew several times over. Treat results on images from other labs, stains or scanners with caution.
  • Calibration makes the probabilities more honest on the training distribution. Under dataset shift the confidence is only a weak signal of which answers are wrong, so referral helps modestly.
  • The ensemble averages the calibrated probabilities of every available model. Averaging improved calibration in the study but not discrimination.
  • The heatmap shows which regions influenced the prediction. It is not a diagnosis and can highlight irrelevant areas.
  • Uploaded images are processed in memory and are not stored.
